Title: Creatine Monohydrate Supplementation
Source: ISSN Position Stand — Creatine Supplementation and Exercise (Buford et al., 2007); updated safety & efficacy review (Kreider et al., 2017)
URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2048496/
DOI: 10.1186/1550-2783-4-6
License: CC BY (Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition)
Tags: creatine; supplements; strength; power; lean mass; loading; safety

Summary (evidence-based position of the International Society of Sports Nutrition):

1. Creatine monohydrate is the most effective ergogenic nutritional supplement currently available for increasing high-intensity exercise capacity and lean body mass during training.

2. Creatine monohydrate supplementation is not only safe but may be beneficial for injury prevention and the management of certain medical conditions when taken within recommended guidelines.

3. There is no scientific evidence that short- or long-term creatine monohydrate use harms otherwise healthy individuals. Long-term studies (up to 30 g/day for 5 years) report it is safe and well tolerated.

4. Creatine monohydrate is the most extensively studied and clinically effective form for muscle uptake and improving high-intensity exercise capacity. Newer "advanced" forms are not proven superior.

5. Adding carbohydrate, or carbohydrate plus protein, to creatine increases muscle creatine retention, though the performance benefit over creatine alone may be small.

Dosing:
- Fast method ("loading"): ~0.3 g/kg/day for 5–7 days (e.g., ~5 g taken 4×/day), then 3–5 g/day to maintain elevated stores.
- Slower method (no loading): 3 g/day for ~28 days reaches similar muscle stores; the ergogenic effect just appears more gradually.

Common myths that are NOT supported by evidence:
- "All weight gained is just water." (Lean mass gains are real.)
- "Creatine causes kidney damage / renal distress." (Not in healthy individuals.)
- "Creatine causes cramping or dehydration." (Not supported.)

Practical takeaways:
- Creatine monohydrate, 3–5 g/day, is a cheap, safe, well-evidenced staple for strength/power and muscle.
- Loading is optional — it just fills muscle stores faster. Daily timing doesn't matter much; consistency does.

Disclaimer: General fitness/nutrition education, not medical advice. Consult a physician if you have kidney disease or other medical conditions.
